# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone. Be respectful,
constructive, and considerate; accept responsibility, focus on what is best for the
community, and show empathy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized
attention, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently exclude participants for inappropriate behavior. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository maintainers. This policy is adapted from Contributor
Covenant 2.1.
